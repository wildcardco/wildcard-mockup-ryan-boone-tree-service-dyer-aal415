The Job Sheet

What we cut, clear, and haul

  • 01 Tree Removal

    Full takedowns from the crown to the trunk — including large and hazardous trees leaning over houses, garages, and power drops. Rigged, dropped, and hauled clean.

  • 02 Trimming & Pruning

Who's Showing Up

A local crew, not a call center

Boone Tree Service Dyer is a hands-on tree operation working out of Dyer, Indiana. We're the people on the saws — not a franchise dispatching subcontractors from three states away.

Working in Northwest Indiana means we know what the weather here does to trees: straight-line winds off the lake plain, heavy wet spring snows, summer storms that split old silver maples down the middle. We've seen how it fails, and we know how to take it down before it takes something with it.

Our approach is simple: look at the job, tell you straight what it needs and what it costs, then show up and do it. No corporate overhead, no upsell script — just the work.
